Important Notes

  • Weekly schedule: Sat Legs · Sun Push · Mon Pull · Tue rest (football) · Wed Lower · Thu Upper · Fri rest. Every muscle 2×/week with 3-4 days between, both rest days off the back of a leg day, and 48 h between heavy squats and deadlifts.
  • Progressive overload: add a little weight or a rep whenever you hit the top of the rep range with good form — aim to beat last session's numbers (shown on each card).
  • Train close to failure: stop most working sets 1-3 reps before failure; on isolation moves (raises, curls, extensions, calves) push to 0-1.
  • Warm-up: 5-10 minutes of light cardio, then 1-2 light ramp-up sets before the first heavy lift of the session.
  • Recover: 7-9 h sleep — muscle is built between sessions. Football counts as leg load, so keep it to Tuesday and off the day before Legs.
  • Deload: every 5-8 weeks take an easy week (about ⅔ the weight or half the sets) to clear fatigue and keep progressing.
  • Protein: 1.6-2.2 g per kg of body weight daily, spread across 3-5 meals.
  • Your data is saved on your device only — no server, no one else sees it. Tap Back up progress every week or two and keep the file in iCloud Drive so a cleared browser or a new phone can't wipe your history.
