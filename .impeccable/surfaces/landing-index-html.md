## Scope
Etqadem / اتقدم marketing landing at the site root (`landing/index.html`, served at `/`), mode **Persuade**. The web app moves to `/app/`. Lives inside the app's established world (dark ink/slate, Deep Teal light source, rose/violet women's plan); adds Alexandria as the landing's own face.

## Audience, job, action
Beginner-to-intermediate lifters in Egypt and the Gulf, Arabic and English as equals. They must understand in one viewport that one free app runs their gym day (plan, set logging, rest timer, form videos, food, an AI coach that answers in Arabic) and tap Start free (opens `/app/` sign-in). Proof = real app screens. No stats, testimonials, ratings, store badges; InBody not the centre; never presented as paid.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The category standard (ElCoach, Nike Training Club, Fitbod) done at full craft: the product proves itself on real phone screens, and the one thing competitors cannot show is a coach replying in Gulf/Egyptian-register Arabic. Refuses invented social proof and orange.
OWN-WORLD: Ink-night ground (#0d1117 family), slate panels, one Deep Teal light source (#0B7A75 fill, #3CC2B8 lit text), Teal to Emerald hero gradient on the primary only; rose #e85c9a / violet #b06cf0 only where the women's plan is shown. Alexandria for Latin and Arabic, 800 display / 400 body. Real phone frames holding real app screenshots; one monochrome lifter photo graded into the ink.
STORY: Visitor sees a lifter and the app mid-workout, reads "plan, log and coach in one free app, Arabic or English", sees the coach answer in Arabic, scans the shipped feature set, taps Start free.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Nav (logo + bilingual wordmark, Features / AI coach / Ramadan, language toggle). Start column: 2-line display headline, one-line other-language echo, 20-word subtext, Start free (gradient) + See how it works (ghost). End column: graded lifter photo field with two overlapping phones (workout screen front, Arabic coach chat behind), visible without scrolling at 1440 and 390.
SIGNATURE INTERACTION: the language toggle flips the whole page (copy, direction, phone screenshots) in place without reload; the coach section's Arabic reply types in once on view (static under reduced motion).
FORM: category standard (canon), user-confirmed shape brief 2026-10-01; no seed key (canon). Code-led; approved mock Gym_repo/.playwright-mcp/etqadem-mock.png is the critique reference.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
